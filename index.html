Frequently Asked Questions

Botox questions, answered

Clear, honest answers to help you make well-informed decisions about your care.

Our approach aims for subtle changes that suit your face. Dosing and injection placement are personalized, but individual responses vary and a specific appearance cannot be guaranteed.
You may notice changes within the first few days. Results continue developing over the following weeks, with full results potentially taking up to 30 days. Effects commonly last around three to four months, although timing varies.
Many people return to routine activities after their appointment. Temporary redness, swelling, tenderness or bruising can occur. Follow your provider’s aftercare guidance and discuss timing if you have an upcoming event.
Your total depends on the areas treated and the number of units recommended. Contact our Bellevue team for current pricing and consultation fees, and discuss your estimated treatment cost before proceeding.
A consultation helps determine whether treatment is appropriate. Tell your provider about your medical conditions, medications, previous injections, allergies, and whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
Possible side effects include bruising, headache, and temporary eyelid or brow drooping. Serious reactions can occur. Seek urgent medical help for difficulty swallowing, speaking or breathing after treatment. Your provider will review contraindications, risks and the product’s safety information with you.
